⚠️  IMPORTED SERIES ARE STALE BETWEEN 15-MINUTE BATCHES. An instant query
    returns EMPTY even when data is landing correctly:

      count(ecobee_zone_temperature_fahrenheit)                    -> empty
      count(last_over_time(ecobee_zone_temperature_fahrenheit[1h])) -> works

    Grafana panels need a step or lookback of at least 15 minutes.

⚠️  THE SECRET IS MUTABLE STATE. Auth0 rotates the refresh token and the
    importer patches the new value back. After the first refresh the cluster's
    copy is authoritative — re-applying a saved copy presents a revoked token
    and locks the account out.
